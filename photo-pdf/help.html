SnapPack — Help

Turn a job's site photos into one dated, ready-to-share PDF — automatically.

What it does

SnapPack adds one button to every job card, Download Photos as PDF. Click it, and SnapPack combines every photo attached to that job into a single PDF, sorted by the time each photo was actually taken — not the order it happened to upload — and saves the finished PDF into that job's Diary.

How to use it

  1. Open any job in ServiceM8 online (the website — SnapPack doesn't currently work in the mobile app).
  2. Click More → Addons → Download Photos as PDF.
  3. Wait while SnapPack loads and reads each photo — a progress bar shows how far along it is.
  4. Review the photos in the grid that appears. Click the × on any photo you don't want included.
  5. Click Build PDF.
  6. Once it shows the PDF has been added to the job's Diary, close the window and open it from there.

Good to know

  • Photos are sorted using the camera's own timestamp where available. If a photo has none, SnapPack falls back to its ServiceM8 upload time and marks the caption "(estimated)" so nothing is misrepresented as more precise than it is.
  • Photos removed from the job in ServiceM8 are automatically left out.
  • Supported photo types: JPG, PNG, and AVIF (the format ServiceM8's own mobile app uses for camera uploads).

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I click the button — make sure you're using ServiceM8 online, not the mobile app.
  • "This job has no photos to include" — SnapPack only looks at image attachments; other file types (PDFs, documents) aren't included.
  • The PDF isn't showing in the Diary yet — refresh the job card. This is a known ServiceM8 display quirk, not a fault in SnapPack.
