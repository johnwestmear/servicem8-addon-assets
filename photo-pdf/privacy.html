SnapPack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

SnapPack is a ServiceM8 add-on developed by Tarrant Building Services Pty Ltd ("we", "us"). This page explains what data SnapPack accesses within your ServiceM8 account when you use it, and what we do with it.

What SnapPack accesses

When you click "Download Photos as PDF" on a job, SnapPack reads:

  • That job's address, purchase order number, and job number — used only to name the resulting PDF file
  • The list of photo attachments on that job, and the image data of each one

It writes:

  • The finished PDF, saved as a new attachment on that same job

SnapPack does not access any other job, client, staff, or account data — only what's needed for the job you actively click the button on.

What we do with it

Photo and job data is processed only for the duration of building the PDF you requested, in direct response to your click. We do not store it beyond that, sell it, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for any purpose other than producing the PDF.

If something goes wrong, operational error logs may briefly include your ServiceM8 account ID and a job reference number. These logs are visible only to us and used solely to diagnose and fix problems — never for any other purpose.

Where your data goes

Photo data passes between your browser and ServiceM8's own servers, and briefly through SnapPack's own function (hosted on ServiceM8's add-on infrastructure) to work around a browser security restriction on direct downloads. It is never sent to any third party.

SnapPack loads two small open-source code libraries (for building PDFs and reading photo metadata) from public content-delivery networks. This downloads program code only — never your data.

Your rights

You can uninstall SnapPack from your ServiceM8 account at any time, which immediately stops it accessing any further data. To request deletion of any operational logs referencing your account, contact us below.
